Delete your Bloomie account

Bloomie (com.bloomie.mobile) — account & data deletion request

You can delete your Bloomie account and the personal data linked to it. Subscriptions purchased through Google Play or the App Store must be cancelled in the store separately.

Option 1 — In the app (fastest)

  1. Open the Bloomie app and sign in.
  2. Go to You → Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

This permanently deletes your account and associated app data (profile, progress, coaching history) from our systems, except limited records we may keep for legal, security, or accounting reasons.

Option 2 — Request by email

  1. Email support@bloomie.app.
  2. Use the subject: Bloomie account deletion request.
  3. Include the email address of the Bloomie account you want deleted.

We will verify ownership and process the request. Deletion is typically completed within 30 days (often sooner).

What we delete

  • Account identifiers and profile details
  • Workout / streak / quest progress synced to your account
  • Coach chat history associated with your account

What may be retained briefly

  • Security / fraud logs needed for a limited period
  • Purchase records required by tax or store policies
