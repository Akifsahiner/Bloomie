Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Bloomie (“we”, “our”, or “us”) operates the Bloomie mobile application (the “App”), a gentle daily movement and coaching product. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

Account and profile

  • Email address and authentication identifiers when you create or sign in to an account
  • Fitness profile details you provide (for example goal, experience, available equipment, limitations, preferred session length)
  • Optional profile display information such as a name or avatar you choose

Activity and progress

  • Workout and session history, streaks, XP, quests, goals progress, and related in-app achievements
  • Coaching interactions and preferences you set inside the App
  • Purchase and subscription status for Bloomie Pro / Premium

Device and technical data

  • Device type, operating system, app version, and approximate diagnostics needed to run the App
  • Crash and performance data, when enabled
  • Push notification tokens if you allow notifications

Automatically collected usage data

  • Feature usage events (for example completing a session, opening a screen) used to improve the product

2. How we use information

  • Provide daily movement plans, coaching, quests, streaks, and personalization
  • Sync your progress across devices when you are signed in
  • Process subscriptions and restore purchases
  • Send optional reminders and engagement notifications you enable
  • Improve reliability, prevent abuse, and respond to support requests

3. Permissions

  • Notifications — optional reminders for movement rituals and coaching prompts
  • Photos / media — only if you choose features that need an image (for example sharing a progress card)

We do not require continuous location tracking or microphone access for core workouts.

4. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with processors that help us run Bloomie:

  • Supabase — account and app data storage / authentication
  • RevenueCat and the app stores (Google Play / Apple) — subscription management
  • Analytics / crash tools (when configured) — product improvement and stability
  • AI coaching providers (when you use coach chat features) — to generate coaching replies via secure server-side calls
  • Legal — when required by law or to protect users and our rights

5. Storage and security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS and stored with reputable cloud providers. Access to production secrets and keys is restricted. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

6. Retention

We keep your information while your account is active and as needed to provide the App. If you delete your account, we delete or anonymize associated personal data except where we must retain limited records for legal, security, or accounting reasons.

7. Your rights

  • Access, correct, or export information associated with your account
  • Delete your account and request deletion of personal data
  • Turn off notifications in system settings
  • Manage or cancel subscriptions through Google Play or the App Store

Contact us at support@bloomie.app to exercise these rights.

8. Children

Bloomie is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under that age.

9. International transfers

Your information may be processed in countries other than your own. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards used by our service providers.

10. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the updated version at this URL and revise the “Last updated” date above.
